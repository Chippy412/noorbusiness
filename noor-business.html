About NICC Business

As-salamu alaykum. We are a small team building the directory our own families kept asking for.

نور

Noor means light.

A directory is a small thing, but finding the right butcher, the right clinic, or the right tutor should not take three group chats and a drive across town. Our job is to make those answers easy to find and honest to trust.

Why we started.

Central Ohio has one of the fastest growing Muslim communities in the Midwest, and the businesses serving it are mostly small, family run, and invisible to the big review sites.

Search for halal food here and you get a national chain three suburbs away before you get the grocer two streets over. We built this directory so proximity, verification, and community trust come first.

What we hold ourselves to.

Verification over assumption

A halal badge means an owner named their supplier and certifier and dated it. If they cannot, the badge does not appear. We would rather show less than imply more.

Open to every neighbor

The directory centers Muslim owned and halal friendly businesses, and everyone is welcome to use it, list on it, and review on it.

No pay to rank

Nobody buys their way to the top of a category. Placement follows relevance, distance, and community ratings.

Owners keep control

You edit your own listing, respond to reviews in public, and remove yourself at any time without emailing support.

Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and how it is kept.

Last updated September 2026. NICC Business is a community-run directory operated by volunteers. This page explains what information we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

If you create an owner account, we store the email address and password you sign up with (through our authentication provider, Supabase) and the business listing details you submit, including any photos you upload. If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email, and message you enter so we can reply, and a one-way hash of your IP address that we use only to limit spam. If you join the email list in the footer, we store your email address. If you save listings while logged in, we store which listings you saved so they follow you between devices. Browsing, searching, filtering, and saving listings do not require an account.

What stays in your browser

Your saved listings and your light/dark theme preference are stored only in your browser's local storage. We do not receive or see this information, and it is never sent to our servers.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use cookies. We use Vercel Web Analytics, a cookieless, privacy-preserving analytics tool that reports aggregate, anonymized traffic patterns (such as which pages are visited) and cannot identify individual visitors.

How data is stored

Account and listing data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, behind access controls that restrict who can read or change it. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Your choices

You can edit or remove your listing at any time from your account, or email us to request that your account and data be deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@noorbusiness.org.

Terms & conditions

The ground rules for using and listing on NICC Business.

Last updated September 2026. By using NICC Business, you agree to the following.

What this directory is

NICC Business is a community-run, informational directory of Muslim owned and halal friendly businesses in central Ohio. It is not a certifying authority. The halal verified badge reflects information an owner has provided about their supplier and certifier, not an independent audit by us.

Listings and accounts

Owners are responsible for the accuracy of the information, photos, and claims they submit. New listings and claims are reviewed by community volunteers before publishing. We may edit, decline, or remove a listing or review that is inaccurate, abusive, spam, or otherwise against the spirit of the directory.

Reviews

Reviews should reflect a genuine visit or interaction with the business. Personal attacks, and content that could not plausibly come from a real visit, may be removed. Business owners may reply publicly to reviews on their own listing.

No warranty

The directory is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind. We do our best to keep listings accurate and current, but we cannot guarantee that every detail is correct at the moment you read it.

Changes

We may update these terms as the directory grows. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated terms.
