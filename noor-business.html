About NICC Business

As-salamu alaykum. We are a small team building the directory our own families kept asking for.

نور

Noor means light.

A directory is a small thing, but finding the right butcher, the right clinic, or the right tutor should not take three group chats and a drive across town. Our job is to make those answers easy to find and honest to trust.

Why we started.

Central Ohio has one of the fastest growing Muslim communities in the Midwest, and the businesses serving it are mostly small, family run, and invisible to the big review sites.

Search for halal food here and you get a national chain three suburbs away before you get the grocer two streets over. We built this directory so proximity, verification, and community trust come first.

What we hold ourselves to.

Verification over assumption

A halal badge means an owner named their supplier and certifier and dated it. If they cannot, the badge does not appear. We would rather show less than imply more.

Open to every neighbor

The directory centers Muslim owned and halal friendly businesses, and everyone is welcome to use it, list on it, and review on it.

No pay to rank

Nobody buys their way to the top of a category. Placement follows relevance, distance, and community ratings.

Owners keep control

You edit your own listing, respond to reviews in public, and remove yourself at any time without emailing support.

Frequently asked questions

How verification works, what listing costs, and how reviews are handled.

For shoppers

The owner has told us which supplier they buy from, which body certifies that supplier, and when they last confirmed it. That record shows on the listing so you can judge it yourself. We are not a certifying authority, and we say so plainly.

Reviews are read by volunteers who live in the area before they publish. We remove personal attacks and anything that cannot plausibly come from a real visit. Owners can reply in public, and their reply sits directly under the review.

For business owners

Listing is free while the directory is growing. If paid features arrive later, existing listings stay free and we will say clearly what changes before anything does.

A volunteer reviews new submissions and usually publishes within two business days. If we need to confirm a halal supplier, it can take a few days longer.
