What the directory is

Noor Business is a community directory of Muslim-owned and halal-friendly businesses in Hilliard, Ohio, run by volunteers for Noor Islamic Cultural Center. Owners create an account, add their business, and a volunteer reads it before it appears. Neighbors can search, save listings, leave reviews and report problems.

Three ideas guide every decision:

  • We are a directory, not a certifier. We do not verify, inspect or certify any business’s halal status, and there is no halal badge. Listings describe a business in its owner’s words, and visitors are told to ask the business directly.
  • Be welcoming. Any business serving the community can list, including halal-friendly places under non-Muslim ownership. Ownership details are shown, not hidden.
  • Be kind and be clear. When you say no, say why. Owners and reviewers are neighbors, not opponents.

Roles

RoleWhat it can do
VolunteerReviews new listings, edits, claims, reviews and reports, and sets badges. Reads contact messages and adds notes.
ModeratorEverything a volunteer can do, plus Featured, taking listings or published reviews down, giving free sponsorships, managing the team, and the Operations section of this guide.

If you are a volunteer and something needs a moderator (for example, taking down a live listing), leave a note on the listing or message a moderator. Every action is checked on the server as well, so buttons you cannot use are simply hidden or disabled.

Turning on two-factor

The Moderation page only works if you are signed in with a second step. Nothing here is visible without it.

  1. Create an account on the site with the email you want to use for the team, and sign in.
  2. Open My account and find the two-factor section.
  3. Scan the code with an authenticator app (any standard one works) and enter the six-digit code to confirm.
  4. Ask a moderator to add your email under Team. Sign out and back in, enter your code when asked, and a Moderation button appears on My account.

Lost your phone? Tell a moderator right away. Do not wait; an account with moderation access is worth protecting.

The decision queues

The first five tabs are queues of things that need a decision. The number beside each tab is how many are waiting. When someone else decides an item, it disappears the next time the page refreshes, so if a button says it was already handled, that is fine.

The reasons field

When you reject, remove, hide or take something down, a box asks why. It is optional but please use it. What you write is shown to the person affected, on their account page and in an email, so write it as if you were speaking to them: short, specific and polite. For example: “We couldn’t find an address or phone number. Add one and send it again.” Do not paste private notes here. Internal comments belong in a listing note (see All listings).

New listings

A person has added a business. Read the whole entry, then approve or reject.

  • Is it a real business or service that serves the community, with a sensible name, category and description?
  • Is there a way to reach it (address, phone or website), or is it clearly online-only?
  • Is the photo appropriate and actually of the business?
  • Does the description make claims we cannot stand behind? Phrases like “certified halal” are the owner’s words, but we do not verify them. If a listing leans heavily on a certification claim, it is fine to approve it as long as it is not presented as ours; if it looks misleading, reject with a reason or ask the owner to reword it.
  • Is it a duplicate of a listing that already exists? Search All listings first.

Approve publishes it immediately. Reject sends it back; the owner sees your reason and can fix it and submit again.

Edits

When an owner changes an already-live listing, the public version stays as it was and the change waits here as a side-by-side comparison: old on the left, new on the right and highlighted. Apply it if the new text is fine. Reject it with a reason if not; the live listing is untouched. Opening hours changes go live straight away and do not come to you.

Claims

Someone says an existing listing is theirs. Approving a claim transfers ownership to that person, so be a little careful.

  • Does the claimant’s email or name plausibly match the business (a domain that matches the website, a name on the website, a phone that answers)?
  • If you are unsure, write to the claimant or the business’s public contact and ask. It is fine to leave a claim waiting while you check.
  • Reject with a reason if you cannot confirm it.

Reviews

Neighbors review businesses. Publish a review if it is an honest account of someone’s experience, even a critical one. Reject a review that:

  • contains personal attacks, hate, threats or private information about named people;
  • is clearly spam, an advertisement, or about a different business;
  • is written by the owner or a relative about their own business, or looks like a competitor’s attack.

A harsh but fair review should stay up. When there are several obvious ones, tick them and use the bulk Publish selected or Reject selected buttons. The reviewer sees your reason, so be polite.

Reports

Visitors can report a listing or a review. Each report shows the reason they picked: closed or moved, wrong information, inappropriate, spam or fake, or something else.

  • Dismiss if the report is wrong or not a problem.
  • Mark fixed if you sorted it out another way, such as editing the listing or contacting the owner.
  • Hide the review or Take the listing down (moderators only) when something must come off the site. The author or owner sees your reason.

If you are a volunteer and a report needs a moderator, add a note on the listing saying what you found, and tell a moderator.

The other tabs

Messages

Everything sent through the Contact form. Use Reply by email to answer from your own mail program, then mark the message as handled so the team knows. Marking one handled shows who did it, and you can undo that. Please do not forward people’s messages outside the team.

Published reviews

The latest 200 reviews that are live. Use this to spot-check what the public sees. Only moderators can remove a published review (the author sees the reason). If you are a volunteer and you spot a problem, tell a moderator.

All listings

Every listing in the directory, whatever its status. Search by name, category, area or owner email, and use the filters for listings with no owner, Featured or Sponsored. For each listing you can:

  • Set badges. Badges are set by the team, never bought or self-assigned: Prayer space, Women owned, Family run and Community service. Use them only when you know them to be true. There is deliberately no halal badge: we do not verify halal status, so we do not put our name behind it.
  • Add internal notes. Notes are only visible to the team. Use them to record what you checked and why, so the next person does not repeat it.
  • See the history. Every decision and note about that listing, and who made it.
  • Change status or Featured (moderators only).

Moderators also see a link to place older listings on the map. Listings are placed on the map automatically when their address changes; the link only catches up ones that were missed. Click it until it says all done.

Activity

A log of the latest 300 decisions and notes, with the name of the team member who made each. It exists so we can answer “who did that, and when?” fairly and without blame. Your own actions appear here too.

Desktop alerts and the count in the tab

The page checks for new items every minute while it is open. New items show a banner and a count in the browser tab. In the left column, Turn on desktop alerts adds a system notification; your browser will ask permission the first time. Refresh now checks immediately.

Featured and Sponsors

Featured

Featured puts a listing in “Featured this month” on the home page. Only moderators set it. It is a decision about community value, such as a new business, a community partner or someone we want to thank, and optionally has an end date after which it stops by itself. It is never for sale through badges.

Sponsors

Paid sponsorship is switched off for now. The masjid is deciding which payment platform to use (Mohid is the one under discussion), and until that is settled owners see the plans marked “Coming soon” and nothing can be charged. Do not promise anyone a date or a price.

Moderators can still give a free placement from the Sponsors tab: pick the listing, the plan and an end date, and add a note for the team saying why. It shows with the same “Sponsored” label. A free placement can be ended early from the same tab. Sponsored listings come first in “Recommended” ordering, but sponsorship never buys a badge or changes a review.

Team management

Moderators manage the team from the Team tab.

  • Add someone: they must already have an account with two-factor turned on. Enter the email they signed up with and pick a role.
  • Change a role with the drop-down and Save role.
  • Remove someone who has stepped back. They keep their account but lose moderation access.
  • The system always keeps at least one moderator, so the last one cannot be removed or demoted. Please keep at least two moderators so there is always a backup.

Only give moderator to people you trust with taking things down and with the server information in the Operations section. When a volunteer leaves, remove them the same day.

Alerts and email

Team members are emailed when something new needs a decision (a new listing, an edit, a claim, a review, a report or a message), as long as email alerts are switched on. If you stop receiving them, check your spam folder first, then tell a moderator; the Operations section explains how email is configured. Owners and reviewers are also emailed about your decisions, which is why the reason you type matters.

Common situations

A spam or fake listing
Reject it (New listings) or take it down (Reports, moderators). Say “This looks like spam” or similar. If the same person keeps returning, add a note on the listing and tell a moderator.
A duplicate
Keep the better, older one. Reject the duplicate with the reason “This business is already listed” and, if the new person is the real owner, point them to claim the existing listing instead.
An owner wants to transfer a listing to someone else
The new person makes an account and files a claim for the listing. Before approving, confirm with the current owner (reply to their message) that they agree. Add a note recording that you did.
A business has closed or moved
If a visitor reported it, check the website or phone if you can. For a closure, a moderator takes the listing down with a kind reason; for a move, ask the owner to update the address or edit it once confirmed.
An abusive or unfair review
Reject it if it is still waiting. If it is live, a moderator removes it with a reason. A review that is merely negative stays. If the owner is angry, explain that we remove abuse, not criticism.
Someone asks us to certify, verify or approve a business as halal
We do not. The directory does not check, inspect or certify halal status, and there is no halal badge. Tell them kindly that listings are in the owner’s words and that customers are encouraged to ask the business directly. For certification, they can contact an appropriate certifying body, or the masjid’s leadership may be able to point them somewhere.
A business owner is upset about a rejection
Reply by email, thank them, and explain what would make it acceptable. Most rejections are about missing information and are easy to fix.
You are not sure what to do
Leave it waiting, add a note, and ask. A short delay is always better than a wrong decision that is hard to undo.

Who to contact

  • For a decision you are unsure about, or a problem with another team member: message a moderator.
  • For anything about the masjid’s policy, payments or the sponsorship decision: the masjid’s leadership, through a moderator.
  • If the site is down or misbehaving, or you think someone else has access to your account: tell a moderator immediately; the Operations section tells them what to check.
  • Visitors can reach us through the Contact form; those messages arrive in the Messages tab.

Thank you for volunteering. Careful, kind moderation is what makes people trust this directory.
